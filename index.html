Why come

Everyone is working out what AI changes about how products get made. Very few get to compare notes with people in the same seat at other companies.

Dscout and DDX are bringing product, design and research leaders together for lunch at Qube Shoreditch. Share what is working, what is not, and who else you should be talking to — and leave with a few names you did not have this morning.
